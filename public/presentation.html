Problem Statement
The Rural Healthcare Referral Crisis
🚨 Critical Gap
📵
Connectivity Blackouts
Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and Sub-Centres frequently experience prolonged power cuts and mobile data outages, disabling cloud-only health registries.
📉
Referral Leakage
Over 42% of referred rural patients drop out between initial frontline screening and hospital arrival due to distance, lack of transport, and zero tracking.
📋
Paper-Based Friction
Frontline ASHA workers maintain physical paper registers. When complications arise, there is no real-time emergency triage or feedback loop on patient outcomes.

Our Solution
Health Saathi 360: Connected Rural Care
💡 Core Pillars
  • 1
    100% Offline-First Capability: Registration forms, clinical screening, and local referral queues work seamlessly without internet, backed by browser IndexedDB and Service Workers.

    Native Bilingual Experience: Full English and தமிழ் interface switcher for frontline workers and medical officers.

Clinical Safety
Rule-Based Emergency Safety Screening
🛡️ Clinical Triage
🚨
EMERGENCY TIER
Triggers: Acute chest pain, respiratory distress, maternal bleeding, altered mental status, infant convulsions.
Action: Call 108 Ambulance immediately; escort to Medical College Hospital.
⚠️
URGENT CARE TIER
Triggers: High fever > 3 days, persistent dehydration, uncontrolled hypertension symptoms.
Action: Community Health Centre (CHC) evaluation within 24-48 hours.
🩺
ROUTINE OPD TIER
Triggers: Mild ailments, chronic medication refill, routine antenatal check-ups.
Action: Local Health & Wellness Centre / Sub-Centre visit.
Clinical Integrity Disclaimer: This rule-based prototype acts strictly as a decision-support guide and does not autonomously diagnose medical illnesses.

Referral Management
Privacy-Preserving QR Referral Slips
📋 Privacy by Design
End-to-End Referral Lifecycle
  • A
    Referral Generation: Unique sequential ID (e.g. REF-2026-001) linked to patient and target hospital.
  • B
    Status Tracking: Real-time status transitions: Pending → Completed → Not Attended → Cancelled.
  • C
    Automatic Follow-up: Setting a referral date automatically schedules an in-app follow-up task.
📱
Zero-PHI QR Code Slip
The QR code encodes only a non-sensitive referral reference token:

{"refId":"REF-2026-001","pid":"PAT-1001"}

Security Guarantee: Medical complaints, symptoms, and phone numbers are NEVER stored inside the QR code or URL parameters.

Care Continuity
Follow-up Care & Social Barrier Tracking
🤝 Patient-Centric
Categorized In-App Follow-up Lists

Frontline staff are notified through structured lists rather than unverified SMS simulators:

📅 Upcoming Visits 🚨 Overdue Appointments ⚠️ Missed Visits ✅ Completed Care
Social Determinants of Health (SDoH)

Captures why patients miss appointments to provide targeted community support:

  • Transport Difficulties: Distance, lack of bus connectivity
  • Economic Loss: Forfeiting daily wages to visit clinics
  • Family Care: Childcare and eldercare burdens
  • Stigma / Anxiety: Apprehension of diagnosis
  • Assistance Recorded: 108 ambulance arranged, ASHA accompanied

System Architecture
Lightweight, Offline-First Engineering
⚡ Minimal Tech Stack
💻
Frontend
Pure HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS. Zero bundlers, zero React/Next.js overhead. Sub-second load time.
⚙️
Backend
Node.js + Express. RESTful API with security headers, JWT role verification, and error sanitization.
🗄️
Database
SQLite (WAL mode). Parameterized queries preventing SQL injection. Under 60MB total memory footprint.
🔄
Offline Sync
IndexedDB local queue + Service Worker shell caching. Automatic idempotent sync upon reconnect.
